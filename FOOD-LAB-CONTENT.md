2. Customer enters name, phone, pickup/delivery, address if delivery, and optional notes.
3. Site opens WhatsApp with a prefilled order message.
4. Customer must review and press Send.
5. Restaurant confirms availability, delivery fee, and timing manually.

No online payment, automatic order acceptance, server-side order storage, or live tracking is implemented.
